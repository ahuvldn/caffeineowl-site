About

Hi, I’m Ahuv, the owl behind Caffeine Owl

While working as a catering consultant, I began crafting menus for restaurants and cafes, and I became utterly captivated by the process. I designed a recipe book for a chef, organised cooking classes, and created event flyers.

Years later, I am certified in Digital Marketing and have expanded my reach: I develop business cards, flyers and manage social media campaigns for independent business owners like I once was when I had my own coffee shop. I’ve also leveraged my personal training background to inspire friends to get fit with customised fitness plans.

Today I’m also an account manager at Fusion Inbound, an inbound marketing agency, where I run blogs, social media and newsletters for clients such as Culligan Des Moines. I look after design and marketing for Kansas City Secrets too. That agency experience shapes how I design: every piece should look good and bring results.
