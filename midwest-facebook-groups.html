Why professionally-designed posts beat AI slop

AI-generated pictures are quick, but customers notice them, and many trust a business less because of it.

Customers want to know

Roughly 90% of consumers want brands to clearly say when an image is AI-generated or AI-modified. [1] [2]

It costs trust

About 32% to 42% of shoppers trust a brand less when it uses AI imagery, and over 70% cite deception as a main concern. [3] [4] [5]

People pay less

Studies show AI-labelled branding or product imagery gets up to 41% lower willingness to pay, and feels less warm. [4]

Real builds trust

Over 80% of people agree that authentic, real photography is vital for building genuine trust in a brand. [1] [6]
